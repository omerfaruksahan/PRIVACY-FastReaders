# Read Fast — privacy policy

This repository exists for one reason: to serve the privacy policy for the
**Read Fast** Android and iOS app (`com.omerfaruksahan.speedreader`) at a public
URL, as Google Play and the App Store both require.

The app's source lives in a separate private repository. Nothing here but the
policy page.

- Published at: <https://omerfaruksahan.github.io/PRIVACY-FastReaders/>
- Page source: [`index.html`](index.html)

## Keeping it true

The policy makes factual claims about what the app does and does not collect.
Those claims have to stay true as the app changes — in particular the
**Advertising** section, which describes the optional rewarded video that
unlocks Premium for 3 hours, and what Google AdMob receives when someone watches
one.

The canonical copy lives with the app source (`mobile/PRIVACY.md` and
`docs/privacy.html`). When it changes there, copy it here as `index.html` and
update the "Last updated" date in both.
